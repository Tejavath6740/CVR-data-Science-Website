Vision

To nurture pioneering Computer Science Engineers with a focus on Data Science by providing them with an outstanding educational experience that fosters all-round development, academic excellence and creative thinking, thereby propelling advancements in technology and society.

Mission

M1: To offer with comprehensive educational program that imparts the academic knowledge with a solid foundation in data science concepts, techniques and applications.

M2: To encourage students for applying the tools, techniques of data analytics and conceptual knowledge for solving societal problems.

M3: To enable researchers and students to discover implicit patterns in large and complex data sets.

M4: To accustom the students with current industry practices, environment and entrepreneurship.

Infrastructure

Computer Lab Infrastructure

The Computer Lab at CVR College provides a well-equipped environment for students to develop practical knowledge and technical skills. The lab consists of computer systems with the required hardware and software for programming, web development, database management, and other academic activities.

The laboratory provides desktop computers, internet connectivity, networking facilities, printers, and essential software tools required for students' coursework and projects. The computers are connected through a network, enabling students to access shared resources and online learning materials.

The lab is maintained with proper power supply, lighting, ventilation, furniture, and security facilities to provide a comfortable and safe environment for students. Regular maintenance and software updates help ensure that the systems remain reliable and ready for academic use.
